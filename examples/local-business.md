# Example: local business website

**Scenario (fictional example, not a client case study):** neighborhood bakery needs a fast, distinctive, mobile-friendly website. Visitors want menu, hours, address, and order or contact path.

## Prompt

Use `build-artful-websites`. Inspect business-provided brand assets, menu, photography, and verified address/hours. Choose a grounded art direction reflecting the bakery rather than default beige luxury, pastel cupcake cards, or animated 3D for its own sake. Write `DESIGN.md`. Make a fast mobile-first site with clear menu navigation, actual product photos, and accessible ordering/contact. If ordering is not implemented, link to a working supported channel and label it accurately. Use reviews only if verified and permitted; no invented counts or ratings. Audit 320–1920px and keyboard/reduced-motion behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- A visitor can find opening hours, address, menu, and action quickly.
- No false ordering/booking affordance or fabricated social proof.
- Editorial visual identity remains distinct with CSS motion disabled.
- Navigable on mobile, keyboard-accessible, images optimized.
- Contact/order journey works end to end.
